FAQ

1What can I do for free?

Manual logging, history, charts, and PRs are always free. You can also scan workouts from photos up to 3 times a month for free.

2What is the OCR Plan?

A monthly subscription that lets you scan workouts from photos without the monthly cap (a daily limit applies). The price is shown on the subscription screen in the app.

3How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions. You can open the same screen from Settings > Manage Subscription in the app. Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

4I got a new iPhone or reinstalled the app, and my subscription isn't showing.

Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen in the app. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

5The app can't read my whiteboard.

Retake the photo in a bright place with the board filling the frame. You can fix anything it read before you save. If it still doesn't work, you can switch to manual entry.

6Where is my data stored? Will it carry over to a new iPhone?

Your workouts are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and they sync across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. If you're not signed in to iCloud, they're saved on that device only. We can't see your workouts.

7How do I switch between kg and lb?

In the app, go to Settings > Units. You can set weight, distance, and height separately. Changes apply across the app right away.

8How do I change the language?

The app follows your iPhone's language and shows English or Japanese. To change it for this app only, go to iPhone Settings > Apps > WOD Logger > Language.

9How do I delete a workout?

Swipe left on it in HISTORY, or open the workout and choose Delete from the "⋯" menu.
