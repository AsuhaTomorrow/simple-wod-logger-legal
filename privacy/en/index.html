1Information We Collect and Use

The App handles only the minimum information necessary to provide the service.

Photos you take (only when using the OCR feature)

When you use the feature that photographs a workout board to fill in entries automatically, that image is sent through our server (Cloudflare Workers) to an AI analysis service (Anthropic) and is used solely to extract text. Images are discarded immediately after analysis and are neither stored nor logged on our server. No image is ever transmitted if you do not use the OCR feature.

Device attestation (App Attest)

To prevent abuse of the OCR feature, we use Apple's App Attest (device integrity attestation). Our server stores only a per-device public key and a usage counter. This is not information that identifies you personally.

iCloud identifier (free-tier management)

To count monthly usage against the free tier, we use an identifier associated with your iCloud account. We store this identifier and the monthly usage count, but we do not collect your name, email address, or other contact details.

Subscription information

If you subscribe to a paid plan, the purchase is made through Apple (the App Store), and to verify that the subscription is active we store on our server the transaction identifier provided by Apple (originalTransactionId) and its expiration date. We never receive or retain payment information such as credit card numbers.

Training records (your data)

The workout content you enter and record is stored on your device and in your own iCloud (Apple CloudKit), and syncs across devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This data remains under your control, and we neither view nor obtain it.

3Retention

  • Photographed images: discarded immediately after analysis (not stored).
  • Device attestation, free-tier counters, and subscription information: retained on our server for as long as necessary to provide the service.
  • Training records: stored on your device and in your iCloud, and retained until you delete them (we do not retain them).

4What We Do Not Do

  • We do not serve advertising or permit third-party behavioral tracking (no tracking SDK is embedded).
  • We do not require account registration or sign-in.
  • We do not sell or rent the information we obtain to third parties.

5Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
